# 💾 Step 2 — Export as STL

<span class="badge badge-3d">3D Printing</span>

Your design lives in TinkerCad right now. To print it, you need to save it as an **STL file** — the format 3D printers understand.

---

## ✅ Your checklist

- [ ] Select everything in your design (Ctrl+A)
- [ ] Click **Export** (top right of TinkerCad)
- [ ] Choose **.STL**
- [ ] Save the file to the **Desktop** — name it `yourname-tag.stl`
- [ ] Tell your teacher — they'll take it from here!

---

## How to export — step by step

!!! note "In TinkerCad"

    1. Click **Export** in the top-right corner
    2. Choose **"For 3D Printing"**
    3. Click **.STL**
    4. When asked where to save — pick **Desktop**
    5. Name your file: `yourname-tag.stl`

---

!!! success "That's your job done!"
    Once you've handed off the file, your teacher will slice it and print it at lunch.
    **You'll collect your tag at 2:45.**
    Head to the [Robot Arm session](../xarm/index.md) next!

---

## What is an STL file?

!!! info "STL = a bag of triangles"
    An STL file describes your 3D shape as thousands of tiny triangles.
    The slicer software reads all those triangles and figures out how to print them layer by layer.
    Curious how that works? Check out [Behind the scenes — Slicing](slicing.md).
