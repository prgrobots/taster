# 🖨️ Behind the scenes — Printing

<span class="badge badge-3d">3D Printing</span>

Your teacher loads and starts all the prints — but here's what the printer is actually doing while you're on the robot arm.

---

## What's happening inside the printer

!!! info "How FDM printing works"
    1. The **nozzle** heats up to about 200°C
    2. It melts the plastic filament (like a super precise hot glue gun)
    3. It traces the shape of **one layer** — 0.24mm thick
    4. The bed drops down 0.24mm
    5. It traces the **next layer** on top
    6. This repeats **hundreds of times** until your object is done

---

## Common things that can go wrong

!!! warning "If something looks odd"
    - **First layer not sticking** — the most important layer. Teacher will sort it.
    - **Stringy bits on the print** — normal! They peel off easily when it's done.
    - **Print stops early** — tell your teacher.
