# ✏️ Step 1 — Design your name tag

<span class="badge badge-3d">3D Printing</span>

You're going to design a **luggage tag with your name** using TinkerCad — a free, online 3D design tool. No experience needed.

---

## ✅ Your checklist

- [ ] Open TinkerCad and sign in (or use teacher's class link)
- [ ] Start the luggage tag tutorial
- [ ] Add your name to the tag
- [ ] Customise the shape or add something extra
- [ ] Check your design looks right from all angles
- [ ] Tell your teacher you're ready to export

---

## 🛠️ The basic TinkerCad moves

You only need to know 4 things to design today:

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Rotate view** | Right-click + drag |
| **Zoom** | Scroll wheel |
| **Move a shape** | Click it, then drag |
| **Resize a shape** | Click it, drag the white corner handles |
