# Artificial Intelligence career pathways and courses

#### Shape your future in AI

**Be equipped to tackle the evolving challenges of network security and artificial intelligence through these pathways in AI and Cyber Security.**

Cyber threats on Australian networks are an ever-increasing risk. At the same time, the rapid adoption of AI technologies introduces new vulnerabilities and opportunities across all industry sectors.

There is currently a significant skills shortage in this highly specialised sector, making expertise in both AI and Cyber Security critical for the future workforce.
