# Games and Intelligent Systems career pathways and courses

#### Master game design and intelligent systems

**Embark on a dynamic journey where game development meets intelligent systems, equipping you with the technical expertise and collaborative skills demanded by tomorrow’s tech landscape.**

Gain advanced skills in game development, virtual and augmented reality, mobile app design, and intelligent systems using industry tools. Work on real-world projects, collaborate with industry partners, and explore emerging technologies.

This hands-on pathway opens doors to careers at intersection of games and emerging technology.
