# Robotics and Automation career pathways and courses

#### Drive innovation and shape tomorrow

**As technology reshapes the world around us, robotics is opening exciting career pathways for young minds ready to make an impact. From smart machines to automated systems, robotics is driving innovation across industries—from healthcare and mining to space and manufacturing.**

Now is the perfect time to gain practical, future-focused skills in a field that’s growing fast and full of opportunity. Whether you love solving problems with your hands or designing high-tech systems, robotics offers rewarding careers that combine creativity, challenge, and purpose.
